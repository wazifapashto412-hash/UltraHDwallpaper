## What the app does
Browse, download, favourite and set wallpapers. Wallpapers are loaded from the Pexels API.

## Data we collect
The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting SDKs. The developer does not
collect or receive any personal data.

## Data stored on your device
Favourites and recently viewed wallpapers (titles, image links, photographer names) are stored locally in
app storage. Remove them by clearing history, un-favouriting, clearing app data, or uninstalling.

## Third-party services
To show wallpapers, the app connects to **Pexels** (api.pexels.com, images.pexels.com). Pexels receives
standard network request data such as your IP address, as with any web request. See https://www.pexels.com/privacy-policy/.

## Permissions
- **Internet** — load wallpapers.
- **Set wallpaper** — apply a wallpaper you choose to the home/lock screen.
Downloads are saved to Pictures via the system media store; no storage permission is requested.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal data.
